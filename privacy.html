Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. OneSpot is designed to keep your data exclusively under your control.

1. Data Storage & Ownership

OneSpot operates without a central database. All your posts, links, text, and images are stored directly in your personal Dropbox account (within a dedicated App Folder). The app simply acts as an interface to your own data. We do not have access to, read, or process your files.

2. Authentication

Authentication is handled securely via Dropbox OAuth. We only store temporary access and refresh tokens locally on your device to maintain your logged-in session. These tokens are never transmitted to any third-party servers.

3. Information Collection

We do not collect, track, or share any personal information. There are no tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising SDKs integrated into this application.
